About

SpeedLogger is a data-driven web application built as part of the CITS3403 Agile Web Development unit at the University of Western Australia. It is designed to help users improve their typing skills through real-time tests, performance tracking, and friendly competition via leaderboards. Users can monitor their progress over time, analyze their most common mistakes, and share their performance with others.

This project was collaboratively developed by Group 16.
